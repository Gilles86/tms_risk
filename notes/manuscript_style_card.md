# Writing to Gilles's voice (extracted from TMS_paper_v10.docx)

Every edit is written as a **before/after replacement of an existing sentence**,
never as a fresh paragraph. The v10 text is the style reference; this card is
the checklist.

## Vocabulary that must be reused, not paraphrased
"magnitude representations", "choice consistency", "risk attitudes",
"neurocognitive", "the parietal ANS", "representational noise", "perceived
value", "presentation order", "payoff magnitudes", "indifference point",
"regressive biases", "prior expectations".

## Sentence habits
* Connectives that open sentences: **"Critically,", "Importantly,", "Indeed,",
  "Note that", "Thus,", "In summary,", "Moreover,", "Conversely,"**
* Reasoning is enumerated inline: *"This pattern follows from two properties of
  the fitted model. First, ... Second, ..."*
* Section transitions are explicit and forward-looking: *"We now turn to more
  mechanistic computational models of choice to better understand this empirical
  pattern."*
* Statistics inline in parentheses, comma-separated:
  `(r(33)=0.76, p<0.001)`, `(slope from 3.02 [95% CI: 2.73, 3.31] to 2.33 [2.07, 2.61], pBayesian < 0.001)`
* Hedges on interpretation, not on results: "suggests", "may", "in line with",
  "broadly consistent with". Results themselves are stated flatly.
* Long-ish sentences with a colon or dash introducing the quantitative payoff:
  *"In quantitative terms: The interquartile range of presented numerosities ..."*
* First person plural, active: "we fitted", "we introduced", "we found".

## Things to avoid (not in v10)
* bullet lists in Results
* bold for emphasis mid-paragraph
* "notably", "crucially" as filler (he uses "critically" deliberately)
* hedging a result that is credible

## Figure-caption style
Panel letter, then a bolded-in-prose short title, then explanation:
*"B) Noise as a function of magnitude. Fitted representational noise ν increases
sublinearly with objective payoff. Solid lines show ... Shaded areas are 95%
credible intervals of the group-level estimates."*
Always closes by saying what the error bands are.
